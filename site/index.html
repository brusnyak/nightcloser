How the test works

Three steps, no sign-up, nothing to install.

1. Tap and sendThe chat opens with the first message already written.
2. Say what you sellOne sentence, or paste your website address. With no price list at hand it uses clearly labelled sample items.
3. Play the customerAsk what your real customers ask. See how it answers, qualifies and hands over.

What you wake up to

  • A conversation, not a missed messageIt answers within seconds at any hour, in the language the customer writes in. Typical reply in the test: 1 to 4 seconds.
  • Asks one question at a timeShort WhatsApp messages, no forms inside the chat.
  • Quotes only what you typedIt does not invent prices, stock or discounts. If it does not know, it says so.
  • A clean hand-over to youRefunds, complaints, legal or medical questions, or a request for a person: it stops selling and passes the chat to you with the history.
  • A follow-up that does not nagIf the customer goes quiet it checks in twice, once with a booking link, and stops when asked.

Honest limits

  • This is a test line, not a live shop. Nothing is booked, paid or stored as an order.
  • If you send your website address it reads the page text (not social pages, not logins). It can miss things: check anything it quotes.
  • If your team already answers every message within minutes, you probably do not need it.
  • It is a language model: it can make mistakes. Check what it says before relying on it.

Questions

Is it a real person?

No. It is an AI agent and says so. A human can take over when the topic needs one.

What does it cost?

The test is free. If it fits your business, we scope it together and quote before anything starts.

What happens to my messages?

They are sent to an AI model to write the replies. Do not type personal or confidential data. The chats are not published.

Does it work in Slovak?

It answers in the language you write in. English and Slovak were tested; other languages may be rougher.

Which number is this?
